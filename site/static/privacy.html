01 · The short version

Private by default.

Inner Breeze stores your practice data on your device. It does not include advertising SDKs or analytics services, and we do not sell personal data. Data leaves your device only when you access the web version or ask the app to use an online or operating-system feature, such as sync, Lumi's online AI, feedback, friends, push notifications, a download, a purchase, or Health Connect.

02 · Data on your device

What stays local.

Depending on the features you use, Inner Breeze may store the following on your device:

  • Breathing and meditation sessions, including dates, durations, rounds, mood or wellness check-ins, notes, and tags you add.
  • Habit names, schedules, colors, counters, completion days, and reminders.
  • Break-reminder settings, activity/idle timing state, and break statistics.
  • App preferences, downloaded audio, cached social results, and import or export state.
  • Lumi conversation history and online AI agreement; your provider configuration and API key if you set up your own provider.
  • If you enable sync, your account identifier, public and private key material, server address, and authentication state.

You can use the app's data controls, your browser's site-data controls, or your operating system's app settings to remove local data. Backups you exported remain wherever you saved them.

03 · Optional sync

Sync only when you connect it.

If you connect the default sync service or a compatible self-hosted server, Inner Breeze sends the information needed to create and maintain your sync account:

  • A pseudonymous public account identifier derived from your sync key, your public key, a client identifier, and authentication or anti-replay metadata.
  • Your synced sessions and their timing or wellness details, habits and completion history, and related non-secret settings.
  • Technical sync state, such as record identifiers, timestamps, versions, and deletion markers.

Sync traffic uses HTTPS. Current clients also create encrypted copies of supported practice records using key material derived on your device. Because compatibility and migration data may also be sent to the server, you should not treat sync as guaranteeing that every field is end-to-end encrypted.

Your private key is not sent during normal sync.

It remains on your device unless you export it. If you use the web deletion tool, the exported key is sent over HTTPS only to prove ownership and complete the deletion request.

A self-hosted server is operated under its owner's terms and privacy practices. Its operator—not Waozi—controls that server's logs, retention, security, and deletion process.

04 · Optional social features

Friends are opt-in.

When you create an alias or use friend features on a connected sync server, the server processes your alias, account identifier, friend requests and connections, and the practice statistics needed for friend leaderboards. Your alias and the progress shown by these features may be visible to people you connect with. Friend lists, requests, and leaderboard results may be cached on your device.

05 · Browser extensions

Permissions with a narrow purpose.

The browser extension uses browser storage for settings and reminder state. Depending on the browser build, it may use alarms, context menus, notifications, and the browser's active-or-idle status to schedule local reminders and quick actions.

Inner Breeze does not read your browsing history, page contents, form data, keystrokes, or mouse position. The browser extension requests host access to api.waozi.xyz for optional sync with the default server. You can leave sync off or choose a compatible server. A custom server must allow requests from the browser extension.

Consistent with the Chrome Web Store Limited Use requirements, extension data is used only to provide or improve user-facing Inner Breeze features. It is not used for advertising, creditworthiness, or unrelated purposes.

06 · Connected services

What happens when you connect another service.

Lumi's optional online AI

Lumi starts offline. Online AI requires agreement in the app. Requests include your message, up to three earlier chat messages, and a limited summary of your habits, tasks, settings, open screen, running practice and local time. Information Lumi reads to answer you, such as a diary entry, may also be sent. The agreement lists the context and limits before you choose to connect.

With Inner Breeze's service, requests go over HTTPS to api.waozi.xyz through Cloudflare, then to Z.ai's GLM model. Z.ai does not receive your Inner Breeze account ID or your IP address from this route. Its terms govern what it retains. Inner Breeze's service does not store or log AI message, context or answer text; it keeps daily request counts with your account ID and date.

With your own compatible provider, chat requests go directly from your device to the provider you configured, with your API key. The key and provider agreement remain on this device. That provider's privacy and retention terms apply. Diary spelling checks and habit icon suggestions still use Inner Breeze's service, sending the text needed for the requested task and counting these requests per account and day.

You can switch Lumi offline or withdraw agreement in Lumi's settings. The service agreement syncs to your account's other devices; your own provider's configuration and agreement stay local.

Feedback and Telegram

Feedback you confirm is sent with your account ID and app, device and system details so the developer can read and answer it. If you link Telegram, messages you send Lumi there are kept by Inner Breeze's service too. These records remain until you delete your account; a developer's copy may remain afterwards. Service backups are deleted within 30 days. Telegram also handles messages under its own terms.

Health Connect

On supported Android devices, you can grant permission to export completed session start and end times and the practice type to Health Connect. Inner Breeze requests write access; it does not use Health Connect to read your other health records.

Push notifications

If you enable push on Android, your chosen provider—such as a UnifiedPush distributor or Google Firebase Cloud Messaging—may process an app/device registration token or endpoint and delivery information under its own privacy terms.

Purchases and project support

Optional purchases, or voluntary support through the project's links, are handled by the store, payment service, wallet, or website you choose. Those providers process payment and transaction data under their own terms. Their records are separate from Inner Breeze sync data.

Website and downloads

Opening the website, fetching app resources, or downloading audio makes standard network requests. Hosting and font providers may receive technical data such as your IP address, browser details, requested URL, and request time.

07 · Retention and deletion

Delete each copy where it lives.

  • Local data: remove it with Inner Breeze's data controls or your device/browser settings.
  • Default sync service: use Clear Remote Data in the app or Delete Sync Account.
  • Self-hosted sync: contact that server's operator or use its deletion process.
  • Connected services and exported files: manage them with the relevant provider or delete the files yourself.

When you clear remote data or delete your sync account, the default sync service stops using it immediately: it no longer syncs, counts toward storage, or appears to others. We keep it for 30 days so you can restore it with the same key, then delete it permanently, including from backups. Items you delete, and photos in Photos' Trash, stop counting toward storage at once and are permanently deleted within 30 days. Payment and Dew records may be kept longer where accounting or law requires. To have your data deleted sooner, contact us.

Security and infrastructure logs may be retained for a limited period where reasonably necessary to operate and protect a service or comply with law. Deleting one copy does not automatically delete copies held elsewhere.

08 · Your choices

You remain in control.

You can use the core app offline, leave sync disconnected, keep Lumi offline, withdraw online AI agreement, avoid creating an alias or adding friends, decline notification and Health Connect permissions, choose not to purchase, export your data, and delete local or remote copies. Children should use optional online features with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required by applicable law.
