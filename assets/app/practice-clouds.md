# Practice sky observations

The two cloud-density layers in `practice-clouds.rg` come from the public-domain photograph *Fair Weather Cumulus Clouds* by R. F. Kresge, NOAA, 1977. [NOAA photograph and attribution](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cumulus1_-_NOAA.jpg)

The texture contains interleaved 8-bit linear red and green channels at 256 × 256 pixels. The source crops are `1120x500+405+22` and `1230x320+485+550` from the original 1804 × 1200 image. A red-minus-blue colour key extracts cloud density, with a short edge fade for seamless horizontal drift.

The stars use measured positions and visual magnitudes from the ESA Hipparcos Main Catalogue, projected around Orion. [Hipparcos Main Catalogue](https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/viz-bin/cat/I/239)
