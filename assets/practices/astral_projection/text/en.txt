[astral_text_1]
Welcome to guided astral projection. Settle somewhere comfortable with your body fully supported. This practice explores floating through imagination. Let your eyes rest closed if you like. You can move, open your eyes or stop whenever you wish.
---
[astral_text_2]
Feel the surface beneath you. Soften your forehead, your jaw and your shoulders. Let that ease travel through your arms, belly, legs and feet. There is nothing to achieve. Give yourself time to settle.
---
[astral_text_3]
Notice your natural breathing. Let each breath arrive and leave in its own rhythm, without holding or forcing it. When thoughts appear, gently come back to the feeling of breathing and the support beneath you.
---
[astral_text_4]
Imagine resting on a small boat on a quiet blue-green sea. A gentle tide carries the boat. Your body stays supported and at rest. In your imagination, allow a sense of lightness to grow.
---
[astral_text_5]
If it feels comfortable, imagine your point of view floating a little above your resting place. Picture the room softly, like a dream. Nothing needs to happen. Let any sensations pass, and return to ordinary breathing whenever you wish.
---
[astral_text_6]
Rest in this imagined space. You might picture drifting toward an open sky, or simply staying where you are. Keep the scene gentle and unhurried. When attention wanders, let it return to the quiet floating image.
---
[astral_text_7]
Begin to let the imagined scene dissolve. Feel the surface holding your body again. Notice your hands, your feet and the air around you. Listen to the sounds of the room. Take your time returning to these simple sensations.
---
[astral_text_8]
Let your breathing become a little fuller, without effort. Gently move your fingers and toes. Stretch if you wish. When you feel ready, open your eyes. Rest here for the final quiet moments, aware of the room around you.
---
