[workout_tip_jumping_jacks]
Jump your feet wide as your arms sweep overhead, then jump back together.
---
[workout_tip_wall_sit]
Back flat against the wall, knees bent at a right angle. Hold still.
---
[workout_tip_push_ups]
Keep your body in one straight line, lower your chest, then press up. Knees down is fine.
---
[workout_tip_crunches]
Feet flat, reach toward your knees as your shoulders curl up, then lower slowly.
---
[workout_tip_step_ups]
Step up onto a sturdy chair or step, stand tall, then step back down.
---
[workout_tip_squats]
Feet hip-width apart. Sit your hips back and down, chest up, then stand.
---
[workout_tip_triceps_dips]
Hands on the edge of a sturdy chair. Bend your elbows to lower, then press up.
---
[workout_tip_plank]
Elbows under your shoulders, body straight from head to heels. Keep breathing.
---
[workout_tip_high_knees]
Run in place, lifting each knee to hip height.
---
[workout_tip_lunges]
Step forward and lower until both knees bend at a right angle. Alternate legs.
---
[workout_tip_push_up_rotation]
Do a push-up, then turn and reach one arm to the ceiling. Alternate sides.
---
[workout_tip_side_plank]
Hand under your shoulder, hips lifted in a straight line. Switch sides halfway.
---
