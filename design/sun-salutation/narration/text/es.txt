[sun_salutation_voice_mountain_start]
De pie en la postura de la montaña, exhala.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_salute]
Inhala y lleva los brazos por encima de la cabeza.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_fold]
Exhala y flexiona el tronco hacia delante.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_half_lift]
Inhala y sube a media altura, espalda larga.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_plank]
Exhala y lleva los pies atrás a la plancha.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_plank_hold]
Inhala y mantén, el cuerpo recto.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_low_plank]
Exhala y baja despacio.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_upward_dog]
Inhala y abre el pecho.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_downward_dog]
Exhala y lleva la cadera arriba y atrás.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_step_forward]
Inhala, da un paso adelante y sube a media altura.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_rise]
Inhala y sube, con los brazos por encima de la cabeza.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_mountain_end]
Exhala y vuelve a la postura de la montaña.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_breathe_in]
Inhala.
---
[sun_salutation_voice_breathe_out]
Exhala.
---
