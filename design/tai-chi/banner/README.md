# Tai Chi practice banners

`taichi/banner-light.png` and `banner-dark.png` in
[practices](https://github.com/waozixyz/practices) are the
central 2:1 band of these 1536×1024 sources, scaled to 1024×512 with a
256-colour palette (octree, which keeps the prayer flags' colours), like
the other practice banners.

Made with Codex's built-in image generation (2026-10-14):

- `light-source.png` from the earlier hilltop banner (style and the monk in
  Single Whip) and the monk's turnaround (`characters/monk/source/turnaround.png`),
  with a setting of its own so it does not repeat Sun Salutation's hilltop
  sunrise: the stone courtyard of a Tibetan monastery with prayer flags, a
  white stupa and snowy Himalayan peaks over a misty valley, early morning.
- `dark-source.png` from `light-source.png` and the Meditation night banner:
  the same scene at night, with butter lamps in the windows and at the stupa.
