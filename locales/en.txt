[tutorial_step_intro]
This breathing practice is based on the Wim Hof Method.
It can be powerful. Use it with care.
Practice sitting or lying down.
---
[tutorial_step_method]
Simply follow 3 steps:

1. Breathe in and out 30 times.
2. Exhale and hold.
3. When you feel the urge to breathe, breathe in and hold for 15 seconds.

Exhale, then repeat the process.

Use the wrench icon %i to adjust rounds, breaths, speed, and pauses.
---
[tutorial_step_breathe]
Breathe in and out 30 times.

Find a rhythm that feels natural to you: steady and quick, while still breathing fully.
---
[tutorial_step_exhale_hold]
After 30 breaths, exhale and hold as long as you can.
---
[tutorial_step_exhale_hold_hint]
When you feel the urge to breathe, press the BREATH button.
---
[tutorial_step_inhale_hold]
Inhale fully and hold for 15 seconds.
---
[tutorial_step_inhale_hold_hint]
Exhale, and the next round will begin.

[lumi_agreement_title]
Use Lumi online?
---
[lumi_agreement_intro]
Offline, Lumi understands requests on this device and sends nothing. Online, it asks an AI model when it can’t understand you on its own.
---
[lumi_agreement_sent]
**What is sent.** Your message, up to three earlier chat messages and a summary of Inner Breeze: up to eight habits with their schedules, questions and last two weeks, the names of paused habits, up to eight tasks, your settings, the open screen, a running practice and your local time. When Lumi reads or changes something to answer you, such as a diary entry, that is sent too. A diary spelling check sends the entry being checked, and habits without an icon send their names and descriptions so the AI can choose one.
---
[lumi_agreement_where_node]
**Where it goes.** To Inner Breeze’s server (api.waozi.xyz, through Cloudflare), which adds Lumi’s instructions and passes it to Z.ai’s GLM model. Z.ai gets no account ID and not your IP address; its own terms decide what it keeps.
---
[lumi_agreement_where_own]
**Where it goes.** Straight from this device to %s, with your API key. That provider’s terms decide what it keeps and logs. Inner Breeze’s server doesn’t see these requests, and your key stays on this device. Diary spelling checks and habit icons still go to Inner Breeze’s server, which doesn’t store or log their text and counts them per day, up to 20, with your account ID and the date.
---
[lumi_agreement_kept]
**What the server keeps.** It doesn’t store or log your messages, the summary, Lumi’s answers or the text of spelling checks and habit icons. It counts all these AI requests per day, up to 20, with your account ID and the date, and keeps these counts.
---
[lumi_agreement_server]
**Feedback and Telegram.** Feedback you confirm with Yes is kept on Inner Breeze’s server with your account ID and your app, device and system details until you delete your account, so the developer can read and answer it; the developer’s copy may remain after that. If you link Telegram, what you send Lumi there is kept the same way. Server backups keep copies for about a month.
---
[lumi_agreement_safety]
**Safety.** Lumi can be wrong. It acts only through Inner Breeze’s own tools, which change habits, tasks, the diary and the app’s look as you could yourself, so check what it did. It is not medical advice.
---
[lumi_agreement_choice]
**Your choice.** Requests travel encrypted (HTTPS). Switch Lumi offline in its title bar at any time; feedback then waits on this device. You can withdraw with the wrench in the title bar, and your answer syncs to your other devices.
