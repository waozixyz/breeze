# Changelog

## [1.1.5] - 2026-10-21

- Show everyone with access and suggest friends as you type an invitation.
- Let owners grant full ownership, editing, or read-only access and remove collaborators.
- Update sharing automatically without a Refresh button.

## [1.1.4] - 2026-10-21

- Sharing shows accepted and pending collaborators consistently.
- Editing a shared list preserves tasks beyond the visible screen buffer.
- Undo restores a task with its comment and completion state.

## [1.1.3] - 2026-10-20

- A shared list names who it is shared with, who is still invited, or who shared it with you.
- Share a list with one icon, as in a document; Invitations appear only while some wait.

## [1.1.2] - 2026-10-18

- Maintenance release for Inner Breeze 2.1.36.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-10-17

- Maintenance release for Inner Breeze 2.1.35.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-15

- Share selected lists with existing friends and edit accepted lists together.
- Accept or decline invitations inside Lists, with no app-wide interruptions.
- Share and Invitations open in a compact dialog over the list.
- Compact tabs keep short list names together and scroll when needed.
- Requires Inner Breeze 2.1.34 or later.


## [1.0.6] - 2026-10-13

- Maintenance release for Inner Breeze 2.1.33.

## [1.0.5] - 2026-10-11

- Scrollbars use your theme's colours and show how much of the list is in view.
- Scrolling while you type a new item keeps the keyboard open.

## [1.0.4] - 2026-10-10

- Requires Inner Breeze 2.1.28 or later.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-07

- Keep app labels and content visible after updating Inner Breeze.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-06

- Use Cells consistently in the cell and its independently delivered package.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-06

- Load independently in the updated app host while retaining saved lists.
- Update the list tabs, editor and completion controls with the Lists package.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06
