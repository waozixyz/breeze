# Photos changelog

## [1.0.4] - 2026-10-24

- Download on the current Inner Breeze, which only runs the new app format.

## [1.0.3] - 2026-10-22

- Make Photos available to everyone without enabling beta apps.

## [1.0.2] - 2026-10-21

- Give each achievement its own meaningful icon.

## [1.0.1] - 2026-10-18

- Back from an open photo returns to the gallery and keeps your place.
- Pinning Photos shows its icon in the sidebar and bottom navigation.
- Photos stay unobstructed while the full image arrives, without a loading
  message over the preview.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-17

- Keep your photos in Inner Breeze: add them from your device, and see them
  newest first, by the day they were taken, on every device of your account.
  Each photo is encrypted on your device and kept once, however often you
  add it, on your account's nodes that take photos.
- Open a photo to see it whole, go to the previous or next one, and move it
  to Trash. Photos in Trash can be restored.
- Photos is in beta and not installed by default; add it from More apps once
  Settings › About › Show beta apps is on.
