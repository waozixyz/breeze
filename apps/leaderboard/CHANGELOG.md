# Changelog

## [1.0.6] - 2026-10-20

- Choose among your installed apps. Practice then lists each installed practice in your saved order.
- Other app boards rank earned achievements separately from practice streaks and averages.

## [1.0.5] - 2026-10-18

- Choose any practice you have installed, including Tai Chi and Astral
  Projection, in your Practice order. Practices you haven't installed are
  no longer listed. Inner Breeze 2.1.35 and earlier keep the original four.
