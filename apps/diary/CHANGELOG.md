## [1.2.0] - 2026-10-17

### Changed
- An entry is Markdown. Headings, **bold**, *italic*, links, lists and quotes show styled, and their marks appear on the line you are editing.
- Photos sit inside the entry, where you add them, with text before or after them on their line. Each one is saved to your photo library, the one Photos shows, under the day it was taken, and the entry links to it there. Delete a photo's Markdown to take it out of the entry.
- Show gallery under each day's entry opens that day's photos, which scroll sideways. Tap one to put it in the entry at the cursor.
- Photos from older entries appear inside the entry, after its text.

## [1.1.8] - 2026-10-10

### Added
- Correct the spelling of an entry with one button. Inner Breeze sends the entry to the Waozi node, which returns it with only misspelled words corrected. It uses the same daily online allowance as Lumi.
- Undo and redo buttons, so a correction or any other change can be taken back.

### Changed
- A title bar shows the date in the middle, between the previous and next day. Today and the calendar sit together on the left, and the entry's tools are compact buttons on the right, leaving more room for writing.
- The entry reaches the bottom of the screen; a notice takes room below it only while it is shown.
