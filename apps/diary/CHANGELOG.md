# Changelog

## [1.1.8] - 2026-10-10

### Added
- Correct the spelling of an entry with one button. Inner Breeze sends the entry to the Waozi node, which returns it with only misspelled words corrected. It uses the same daily online allowance as Lumi.
- Undo and redo buttons, so a correction or any other change can be taken back.

### Changed
- A title bar shows the date in the middle, between the previous and next day. Today, the calendar, the time and photos are now compact buttons above the entry, leaving more room for writing.

## [1.1.7] - 2026-10-10

### Changed
- When a photo is not on this device, for example because your sync node does not store photos, the entry shows where the photo is instead of an empty space.

## [1.1.6] - 2026-10-10

### Changed
- Show **bold** text as bold. The ** marks appear only on the line you are editing, so you can still change them.

## [1.1.5] - 2026-10-10
### Changed
- Save your writing quietly, without a "Waiting to save…" note below the entry.

## [1.1.4] - 2026-10-08

- Keep date limits working when Diary updates on existing Inbe installations.

## [1.1.3] - 2026-10-08

- Keep the journal on today or earlier dates and show an empty editor without a writing prompt.
- Open the same account-synced Diary from Inner Breeze and Harmony, preserving earlier entries and photos.

## [1.1.2] - 2026-10-07

- Keep app labels and content visible after updating Inner Breeze.

## [1.1.1] - 2026-10-06

- Use Cells consistently in the cell and its independently delivered package.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-06

- The journal editor and calendar can update independently through the Diary package.
- Attach and browse photos in daily entries, including photos imported from Harmony. Removing an attachment preserves its original private file.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-06

- First independently versioned Diary module.
