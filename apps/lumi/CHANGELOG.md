## [1.4.0] - 2026-10-13
### Added
- Lumi understands habit requests on your device, in every app language: add, rename, pause, resume or delete a habit, set its days and reminder, count it or measure it from start to end, and give it questions with what a good answer is. For example “add a habit push ups every day at 8”, “jumping rope should ask how many I did, higher is better” or “my eating window is good at most 8 hours”. Lumi asks before it deletes a habit.
- A title bar with a connection button that switches Lumi between offline and online, and a wrench that opens Lumi's settings; Back returns to the chat. Lumi starts offline and goes online only after you agree to its online terms, which say what is sent, where it goes and what is kept. Until you answer, Lumi shows them when you open it, once each time Inner Breeze starts.
- Lumi's settings: read or withdraw the online terms, and use your own OpenAI-compatible AI provider with its address, key and model instead of Inner Breeze's server.
- Tap Lumi beside her message and she swirls around, just for fun.
### Changed
- Offline, Lumi sends nothing: no AI requests, no feedback delivery and no Telegram messages until you switch it online.
- Lumi answers a request it understood but couldn't carry out itself, such as a name that matches several items, instead of asking the AI.
- Diary spelling checks and automatic habit icons follow the same switch and terms. Until then the diary offers no spelling corrections, and habits show their practice's icon or their first letter.
- Requires Inner Breeze 2.1.33 or later.
