Added
---
- Chess, an optional app in More apps: play the computer at three levels, or invite a friend from your friend list.
- More apps lists the optional apps your node offers, so new apps arrive without an app update.
- Settings › About › Show beta apps lists apps your node is still testing.
- Photos, a beta app in More apps: add photos from your device and see them by the day they were taken on every device of your account. Each photo is encrypted on your device and kept once, however often you add it. Open a photo to see it whole, move it to Trash, or restore it from Trash.
Changed
---
- Photos, and Diary photos, are copied to every node in your node list whose photo upload is on, not only your sync node, so you can keep your photos on your own node. A device fetches a photo from your sync node first, then from your other nodes.
Fixed
---
- Photos that a camera stored sideways, such as portrait shots, show upright in Diary on desktops.
